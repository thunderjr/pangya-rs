Fixtures contain no client assets or attested real account data. Most values are
synthetic. The exact upstream PangCrypt/PacketDoc `john` vector is a published
dummy test vector whose digest is the well-known unsalted MD5 of the literal
secret `test`; it is neither irreversible nor evidence of a real account. Its
identity is not independently attested, and the bytes are preserved exactly
because the protocol specification requires all three upstream vectors.
GPL/AGPL/unlicensed references were not copied and remain behavior-only. M3 IFF
headers, record sizes, bootstrap layouts, and packet ordering are synthetic and
must not be described as extracted from or accepted by a U.S. 852 client. The M4
`0x7f00`/`0x7f80` opcode families, room layouts, and ordering are likewise wholly
generated local contracts and are not sourced from or accepted by U.S. 852.
